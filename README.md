# CADEN OS • Daily Habits & Rhythm

A luxury, zero-server-cost daily habit matrix, streak engine, and interactive visual analytics dashboard designed for PC and Mobile.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 📊 Interactive Habit Matrix Grid
- **Spreadsheet Checkbox Grid**: Full monthly matrix with week groupings (`Week 1` to `Week 5`), day-of-week indicators (`Su`, `Mo`, `Tu`, etc.), and day numbers (`1` to `31`).
- **Weekly Completion Rings**: Real-time circular percentage progress rings for each week (inspired by top productivity templates).
- **Persistent Horizontal Scroll Memory**: Seamlessly scroll across all weeks and toggle checkmarks without jumping back.
- **Dynamic "Today" Column**: Today's column is automatically highlighted with glowing borders and `NOW` indicator.
- **Tactile Sound & Micro-Animations**: Synthesizer audio feedback on checkmarks, level-ups, and streaks.

### 2. 📈 Charts & Visual Analytics Hub
- **Cubic Bézier Spline Line Graph**: Butter-smooth daily discipline curve with radiant gradient area fills and 80% benchmark target lines.
- **Category Donut / Pie Diagram**: Interactive SVG arc diagram showing energy allocation across Health, Productivity, Mindset, and Evening routines.
- **Weekday Adherence Pattern**: 7-day Monday through Sunday bar comparison crowning the `👑 Best Day` with weekday vs weekend averages.
- **Routine Ranking Matrix**: Gold, Silver, and Bronze tiered leaderboard with progress bars and streak flame badges.

### 3. 🔥 Streaks & Momentum Leaderboard
- **Calendar Heatmap**: GitHub-style consistency activity view showing unbroken momentum chains.
- **Habit Streak Leaderboard**: Interactive streak cards with monthly check-in dot timelines.
- **Viral Social Streak Brag Card**: One-click high-resolution (1200x675) canvas card generator optimized for Instagram Stories, Twitter/X, and WhatsApp.

### 4. ⚡ Overview Command Center
- **Today's Fast Checklist**: Instant 1-click routine execution.
- **Executive KPI Metrics**: Today's progress, active unbroken chains, and monthly consistency.
- **Quick Navigation & Celebrations**: Rapid shortcuts to all views and instant confetti triggers.

### 5. 📲 Cross-Device Sync (Zero Server Cost)
- **100% Private Local Storage**: Dual-snapshot local persistence stored exclusively on user devices.
- **Instant QR Sync**: Scan a QR code on PC with your phone camera to transfer all habits and progress with zero backend.
- **PWA Ready**: Installable as a standalone app on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows.
